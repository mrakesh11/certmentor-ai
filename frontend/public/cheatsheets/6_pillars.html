💡 AWS Well-Architected Framework – 6 Pillars

1️⃣ Operational Excellence

  • ⚙️Focus on operations, monitoring, and continuous improvement
  • 🔄Automate manual processes
  • 🧪Use runbooks, playbooks, and game days

2️⃣ Security

  • 🔐Protect data with confidentiality, integrity, and availability
  • 🧑‍💻Use least privilege access (IAM best practices)
  • 📋Enable auditing, logging (CloudTrail, CloudWatch)

3️⃣ Reliability

  • 🔁Ensure workload recovery, fault tolerance, and failover
  • 🛠️Test recovery procedures regularly
  • 📦Use multi-AZ, backups, and auto-scaling

4️⃣ Performance Efficiency

  • 🚀Use right resources for workload
  • 🌎Adopt new tech like serverless, containers, and caching
  • 📈Monitor performance and adjust based on demand

5️⃣ Cost Optimization

  • 💸Avoid unnecessary costs and pay only for what you use
  • 📉Use Savings Plans, RIs, Auto Scaling
  • 🔍Monitor with AWS Budgets, Cost Explorer

6️⃣ Sustainability 🌱

  • 🌍Minimize environmental impact of cloud workloads
  • 💡Use efficient infrastructure and shared resources
  • 📊Track sustainability improvements over time
