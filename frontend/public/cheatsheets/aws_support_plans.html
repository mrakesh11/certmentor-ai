AWS Support Plans Cheat Sheet

Exam Tip: Understanding the differences between AWS support plans is crucial for the Cloud Practitioner exam. Pay special attention to response times, included services, and access to AWS support tools.

Individual Support Plan Configurations

Basic Support
  • Included free for all AWS customers
  • Access to documentation, whitepapers, and support forums
  • Access to AWS Trusted Advisor - Limited to 7 core checks only
  • Access to AWS Personal Health Dashboard
  • Access to Customer Service & Communities (billing and account questions)
  • No technical support for AWS services
  • No support cases
  • No third-party software support
Developer Support
  • Everything in Basic Support
  • Business hours email access to Cloud Support Associates
  • Unlimited cases / 1 primary contact
  • General guidance response: < 24 hours
  • System impaired response: < 12 hours
  • No access to Infrastructure Event Management
  • No architectural guidance
  • Intended for experimentation and development use cases
  • Starts at $29/month (minimum)
Business Support
  • Everything in Developer Support
  • 24/7 phone, email, and chat access to Cloud Support Engineers
  • Unlimited cases / unlimited contacts
  • Access to full set of Trusted Advisor checks
  • Production system impaired response: < 4 hours
  • Production system down response: < 1 hour
  • Context-specific architectural guidance
  • Third-party software support for common operating systems
  • Access to Infrastructure Event Management for additional fee
  • Intended for production workloads
  • Starts at $100/month (minimum)
Enterprise On-Ramp Support
  • Everything in Business Support
  • Access to a pool of Technical Account Managers (TAMs)
  • Consultative review for your AWS use case
  • Business-critical system down response: < 30 minutes
  • Access to proactive guidance via TAMs
  • Access to Concierge Support Team
  • Access to one Infrastructure Event Management per year
  • Intended for production/business critical workloads
  • Starts at $5,500/month (minimum)
Enterprise Support
  • Everything in Enterprise On-Ramp Support
  • Designated Technical Account Manager (TAM)
  • Business-critical system down response: < 15 minutes
  • Application architecture guidance - consultative review and guidance
  • Infrastructure Event Management, Well-Architected reviews, workshops included
  • Access to online training with self-paced labs
  • Access to management business reviews
  • Intended for mission-critical workloads
  • Starts at $15,000/month (minimum)

Key Exam Focus Areas:
  • Response time SLAs across different support tiers
  • The difference in Trusted Advisor access (7 core checks vs full set)
  • TAM availability (none, pooled, or designated)
  • Infrastructure Event Management availability
  • Third-party software support starting at Business tier
  • Which plans offer architectural guidance and to what extent
