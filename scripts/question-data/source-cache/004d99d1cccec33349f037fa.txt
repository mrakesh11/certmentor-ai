 What is Amazon SES?
 Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon SES) s a fully managed, cloud-based email sending and receiving service that integrates into any application through an API or SMTP interface. It supports high-volume transactional, marketing, and newsletter email for developers and businesses, with dedicated, shared, or owned IP address options and pay-for-what-you-use pricing.

 What problems does Amazon SES solve?
 Poor inbox placement and deliverability
 Amazon SES improves inbox placement without guesswork on sender reputation. Virtual Deliverability Manager and IP management tools help you reach more inboxes as a trusted sender and monitor deliverability across campaigns. 
 Complex email infrastructure setup
 Amazon SES simplifies email configuration without stitching together separate sending tools. Mail Manager protects and automates incoming email while giving you security and compliance control over what you send. 
 Sending email from inside applications
 Amazon SES delivers transactional and promotional messages without a separate email platform. Configure SES directly within your application to send purchase confirmations, shipping notices, and status updates programmatically. 
 Limited visibility into email performance
 Amazon SES reports on sender statistics and deliverability without external analytics tooling. Built-in reporting helps businesses measure results so every email counts toward customer engagement.

 Frequently asked questions
 Who is Amazon SES best for? 
 Amazon SES is best for developers and businesses that need to send high-volume transactional, marketing, or newsletter email from their applications. It suits teams that want reliable delivery, flexible IP options, and pay-as-you-go pricing without building or maintaining their own email servers.
 What are the benefits of Amazon SES? 
 Amazon SES delivers proven scale of more than a trillion emails per year, flexible dedicated, shared, or owned IP deployment, and built-in deliverability tooling. You pay only for what you send, with reporting on sender statistics and integrated inbound and outbound email management.
 How does Amazon SES improve email deliverability? 
 Amazon SES improves deliverability with Virtual Deliverability Manager and IP management tools that help you send as a trusted sender. These features surface inbox placement insights and let you manage sender reputation so more of your email reaches customer inboxes.
 What types of email can I send with Amazon SES? 
 You can send transactional email such as purchase and shipping notifications, marketing email including campaigns and promotional offers, and newsletters. Amazon SES integrates into any application through an API or SMTP interface to send both transactional and promotional messages at scale.
 Does Amazon SES support receiving email? 
 Yes. Amazon SES receives incoming email and includes Mail Manager, which lets you protect and automate inbound email while applying security and compliance controls. This gives you a single service for both sending and receiving email at scale.
