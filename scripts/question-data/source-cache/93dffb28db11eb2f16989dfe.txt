 FAQs
 What is Amazon Connect Customer?
 Amazon Connect Customer is a purpose-built agentic AI solution for customer experience. It powers the full lifecycle of customer interactions, from proactive outreach to self-service resolution to agent assistance, on any channel and at enterprise scale. Most businesses know their customer experience should be better but are held back by systems that route and deflect instead of resolve. Connect Customer changes that: its AI acts on the customer's behalf and resolves what they came for, running on the same AWS infrastructure that handles millions of daily interactions.
 What is agentic AI for customer service?
 An approach to AI that reasons, decides, and acts autonomously rather than following a script or waiting for triggers at every step. In Amazon Connect Customer, business users design these agentic experiences on a no-code visual canvas, blending agentic reasoning with deterministic precision in one governed flow. Conversations stay natural, while steps that must go exactly right, like identity verification and compliance disclosures, run as structured, rule-based steps.
 What is an AI agent for customer service, and what can it do?
 An AI agent autonomously handles customer interactions from start to finish. It understands intent, maintains context across channels, retrieves knowledge, and takes action such as processing a return, updating an account, or rebooking a flight. AI agents can also work across voice and screen at the same time, so a customer talks through a request while seeing and confirming the details in front of them. With Amazon Connect Customer, AI agents resolve customer requests and work alongside human agents in real time, providing step-by-step guidance and suggested responses so customers get what they need faster and at lower cost.
 What channels does AI-powered customer service support?
 Amazon Connect Customer delivers the same AI, routing, and customer context across every channel - voice, chat, email, SMS, web, WhatsApp, Apple Messages, and more. Customers move between channels without repeating themselves. Live Sync goes further: the only native technology that synchronizes a voice conversation with an on-screen interface, so customers see options, fill forms, and complete transactions while they speak. Adding a new channel takes configuration, not a new project.
 How does Amazon Connect Customer’s pricing work?
 Connect Customer uses pay-as-you-go pricing with no minimums, no long-term contracts, no seat-based licensing, and no token-based charges. You pay per interaction by channel: voice is $0.038 per minute, chat is $0.010 per message, SMS is $0.014 per message, and email is $0.080 per email. All AI capabilities are included in the channel price, so there are no separate AI license fees. Costs scale with actual usage, so businesses innovate with AI without new business cases or financial approvals for each capability.
 Is AI-powered customer service secure and compliant?
 Yes. Amazon Connect Customer keeps data, models, and conversation logic within one AWS trust boundary. HIPAA eligible, with support for PCI DSS, FedRAMP, SOC, ISO 27001, and GDPR. All data encrypted at rest and in transit, stays in your chosen AWS Region. Every AI reasoning step is governed per turn, every answer auditable, and controlled by role-based permissions - defining who builds, which models are approved, and what the AI is allowed to say.
 How long does it take to launch AI-powered customer experience?
 Connect Customer turns what used to take months into weeks. Business and CX teams design conversational experiences directly on a visual no-code canvas, with no engineering handoff required for the conversation design itself. Pre-built templates get your first use case live in weeks, not months. Start with a single workflow, prove value, and expand at your own pace. For teams that want full architectural control, a developer-first path gives you the same speed without constraints.
