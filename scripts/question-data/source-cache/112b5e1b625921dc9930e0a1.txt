 Benefits of AWS Systems Manager
 Enhance visibility across your entire node infrastructure 
 Systems Manager provides a centralized view of nodes across your organization’s accounts and Regions. Quickly access node information such as ID, name, operating system details, installed agents, and tags. Use Amazon Q Developer to query node metadata using natural language, helping you identify issues and take action faster. 
 Boost operational efficiency with automation 
 Automate common operational tasks and reduce time and effort required to maintain your systems. Systems Manager provides you safe, secure remote management of your nodes at scale without logging into your servers, replacing the need for bastion hosts, SSH, or remote PowerShell. It provides a simple way of automating common operational tasks across groups of nodes such as registry edits, user management, and software and patch installations. 
 Simplify node management at scale in any environment 
 The Systems Manager Agent (SSM Agent) runs in any environment on AWS, on-premises, and multicloud, making it possible for Systems Manager to provide out-of-the-box visibility and making it simpler to maintain managed nodes. Schedule diagnoses to run automatically to identify SSM Agent issues. You can then remediate issues with pre-defined runbooks. Once managed, nodes can effectively execute critical operational tasks such as patching nodes with security updates, initiating logged sessions, and running commands remotely.

 Use cases
 Manage all your nodes 
 Get comprehensive visibility of your node infrastructure across AWS accounts and Regions as well as your hybrid and multicloud environments. Quickly identify and fix agent issues to remediate unmanaged nodes and effectively execute critical operational tasks, such as patching nodes with security updates, initiating and logging sessions, or running operational commands.
 Automate your operations 
 Provision, configure, and automate the deployment of your compute resources. Keep infrastructure up-to-date with SSM Agent diagnosis and remediation to fix common issues like misconfigured agents. Run critical operational tasks such as automatically deploying operating system and application patches on a regular cadence.
 Drive operational efficiency 
 Focus on driving operational efficiency, reducing costs, and scaling your business. AWS Systems Manager is your enterprise-grade solution for managing nodes at scale, with cross-account and cross-Region visibility across your hybrid and multicloud environments.
