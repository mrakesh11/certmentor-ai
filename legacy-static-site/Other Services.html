Essential concepts and services for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam

AWS Right Sizing

The process of matching instance types and sizes to workload requirements at the lowest possible cost.

  • Principle: EC2 has many instance types, but the most powerful isn't always the best choice due to the elastic nature of the cloud.
  • Best Practice: Scaling up is easy, so always start small.
  • Definition: Identifying opportunities to eliminate or downsize resources without compromising capacity requirements.
  • When to Right Size:
    • Before cloud migration
    • Continuously after onboarding (requirements change over time)
  • Tools that help: CloudWatch, Cost Explorer, Trusted Advisor, and 3rd party tools

AWS Professional Services & Partner Network

The AWS Professional Services organization is a global team of experts that work alongside your team and a chosen member of the APN.

APN (AWS Partner Network)

  • APN Technology Partners: Provide hardware, connectivity, and software
  • APN Consulting Partners: Professional services firms that help build on AWS
  • APN Training Partners: Help you learn AWS

AWS Partner Programs

  • AWS Competency Program: Granted to APN Partners who demonstrate technical proficiency and proven customer success in specialized solution areas
  • AWS Navigate Program: Helps Partners become better Partners

AWS IQ

  • Quickly find professional help for AWS projects
  • Engage and pay AWS Certified 3rd party experts for on-demand project work
  • Features: Video-conferencing, contract management, secure collaboration, integrated billing
  • Designed for AWS customers seeking expert assistance

AWS re:Post

AWS-managed Q&A service offering crowd-sourced, expert-reviewed answers to technical questions about AWS (replaces original AWS Forums)

  • Part of the AWS Free Tier
  • Community members earn reputation points to build their community expert status
  • Questions from AWS Premium Support customers that don't receive community responses are passed to AWS Support engineers
  • Not intended for time-sensitive questions or proprietary information
  • Contains the most frequent & common questions and requests

AWS Managed Services (AMS)

Provides infrastructure and application support on AWS

  • Offers a team of AWS experts who manage and operate your infrastructure for security, reliability, and availability
  • Helps organizations offload routine management tasks to focus on business objectives
  • Fully managed service handling common activities:
    • Change requests
    • Monitoring
    • Patch management
    • Security
    • Backup services
  • Implements best practices and maintains AWS infrastructure to reduce operational overhead and risk
  • AMS business hours: 24/365
