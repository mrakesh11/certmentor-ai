Cloud Practitioner Exam Study Sheet

A comprehensive guide to the CAF Perspectives and Foundational Capabilities

Business Capabilities

Business Perspective

Helps ensure that your cloud investments accelerate your digital transformation ambitions and business outcomes.

  • Strategy Management
  • Portfolio Management
  • Innovation Management
  • Product Management
  • Strategic Partnership
  • Data Monetization
  • Business Insight
  • Data Science
People Perspective

Serves as a bridge between technology and business, accelerating the cloud journey to help organizations more rapidly evolve to a culture of continuous growth, learning, and where change becomes business-as-normal.

  • Culture Evolution
  • Transformational Leadership
  • Cloud Fluency
  • Workforce Transformation
  • Change Acceleration
  • Organization Design
  • Organizational Alignment
Governance Perspective

Helps you orchestrate your cloud initiatives while maximizing organizational benefits and minimizing transformation-related risks.

  • Program and Project Management
  • Benefits Management
  • Risk Management
  • Cloud Financial Management
  • Application Portfolio Management
  • Data Governance
  • Data Curation

Technical Capabilities

Platform Perspective

Helps you build an enterprise-grade, scalable, hybrid cloud platform; modernize existing workloads; and implement new cloud-native solutions.

  • Platform Architecture
  • Data Architecture
  • Platform Engineering
  • Data Engineering
  • Provisioning and Orchestration
  • Modern Application Development
  • Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery
Security Perspective

Helps you achieve the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data and cloud workloads.

  • Security Governance
  • Security Assurance
  • Identity and Access Management
  • Threat Detection
  • Vulnerability Management
  • Infrastructure Protection
  • Data Protection
  • Application Security
  • Incident Response
Operations Perspective

Helps ensure that your cloud services are delivered at a level that meets the needs of your business.

  • Observability
  • Event Management (AIOps)
  • Incident and Problem Management
  • Change and Release Management
  • Performance and Capacity Management
  • Configuration Management
  • Patch Management
  • Availability and Continuity Management
  • Application Management

Memorization Techniques for CAF Perspectives

Mnemonic: B-P-G-P-S-O

"Business People Govern Platform Security Operations"

This sentence helps you remember the six CAF perspectives in a logical order: Business, People, Governance, Platform, Security, Operations

Color Association

Each perspective has its own color in this study guide:

  • Business = Blue (Both start with B)
  • People = Green (People grow like plants)
  • Governance = Yellow (Yellow light signals caution)
  • Platform = Red (Red foundation/platform)
  • Security = Teal (Teal for protection)
  • Operations = Purple (Purple for daily procedures)

Visualize these colors when recalling each perspective.

Platform Capabilities Story

Imagine building a house:

First, you need the Architecture (Platform & Data). Then you need Engineering skills (Platform & Data).

You Provision materials and Orchestrate the construction.

You use Modern Application techniques and ensure Continuous Integration of components with Continuous Delivery of results.

Security Capabilities Grouping

Group them into three categories:

  • Strategy: Security Governance, Security Assurance
  • Prevention: Identity and Access Management, Vulnerability Management, Infrastructure Protection, Data Protection, Application Security
  • Response: Threat Detection, Incident Response

Operations Workflow Memory Hook

Think of these as steps in a workflow:

  1. Observability - You watch the system
  2. Event Management - You notice events
  3. Incident Management - You handle issues
  4. Change Management - You make improvements
  5. Performance Management - You optimize
  6. Configuration Management - You standardize
  7. Patch Management - You update
  8. Availability Management - You ensure uptime
  9. Application Management - You maintain software

First letter mnemonic: "OEICPCPAA" or "Oh, Every Incident Creates Performance Challenges; Patch And Adapt!"

Visual Association: The Cloud Building

Imagine a building that represents your cloud implementation:

  • Foundation (Platform Perspective): The technical base that everything stands on
  • Structure & Walls (Security Perspective): Protects everything inside
  • Utilities & Systems (Operations Perspective): Keeps everything running smoothly
  • Management Office (Governance Perspective): Ensures rules are followed
  • Staff (People Perspective): Makes everything work
  • Executive Suite (Business Perspective): Guides the overall direction
