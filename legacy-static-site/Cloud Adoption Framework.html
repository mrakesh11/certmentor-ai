AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) Cheat Sheet

🎯 Exam Focus

For the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam, you need to understand:

  • The six perspectives of the CAF
  • Which stakeholders are associated with each perspective
  • The primary focus of each perspective
  • How these perspectives help organizations accelerate cloud adoption
  • The four transformation domains that describe areas of business change

What is the AWS CAF?

The AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) provides guidance for coordinating the different parts of organizations that are moving to the cloud. AWS CAF identifies specific organizational capabilities that underpin successful cloud transformations.

AWS CAF Transformation Domains

The CAF identifies four transformation domains that help organizations understand and plan for different aspects of digital transformation:

1. Technology

Using the cloud to migrate and modernize legacy infrastructure, applications, and data platforms.

Key Activities:

  • Migrating existing workloads to the cloud
  • Modernizing legacy applications and infrastructure
  • Implementing cloud-native architectures
  • Creating seamless hybrid cloud environments
  • Adopting containerization and serverless technologies

Example:

A logistics company migrates from on-premises data centers to AWS, refactoring monolithic applications into microservices using Amazon ECS and modernizing their database strategy by moving from Oracle to Amazon Aurora.

Exam Focus: Understand the "6 Rs" of migration (Rehost, Replatform, Repurchase, Refactor, Retire, Retain)

2. Process

Digitizing, automating, and optimizing business operations by leveraging new data platforms and machine learning capabilities.

Key Activities:

  • Digitizing manual business processes
  • Automating repetitive operational tasks
  • Creating data lakes for analytics
  • Implementing ML-based business intelligence
  • Improving customer experiences through analytics

Example:

A healthcare provider builds a data lake using Amazon S3 and AWS Glue, implements analytics dashboards with QuickSight, and uses Amazon Comprehend Medical to extract insights from patient records to improve care protocols.

Exam Focus: Understand how AWS analytics and ML services enable business process optimization

3. Organization

Reimagining operating models, organizing teams around products and value streams, and leveraging agile methods.

Key Activities:

  • Restructuring IT teams around product lines
  • Implementing DevOps practices
  • Adopting agile development methodologies
  • Creating Communities of Practice
  • Implementing continuous learning cultures

Example:

A financial services company reorganizes its traditional IT departments into cross-functional product teams with AWS skills distributed across teams, implements CI/CD pipelines with AWS CodePipeline, and adopts a DevOps culture.

Exam Focus: Understand how cloud adoption impacts organizational structure and culture

4. Product

Reimagining business models by creating new value propositions (products & services) and revenue models.

Key Activities:

  • Creating new cloud-enabled products
  • Developing innovative services
  • Finding new revenue streams
  • Exploring usage-based business models
  • Creating digital experiences for customers

Example:

A media company transforms from traditional broadcasting to offering a direct-to-consumer streaming platform built on AWS, with personalized content recommendations powered by Amazon Personalize and a new subscription-based revenue model.

Exam Focus: Understand how cloud enables business model innovation and new revenue streams

⚠️ Important for the Exam

Understanding the relationship between the six CAF perspectives and the four transformation domains:

  • The six perspectives focus on organizational capabilities needed for cloud adoption
  • The four transformation domains describe areas of business change enabled by cloud
  • Successful transformation requires addressing both organizational capabilities and business change areas

The Six CAF Perspectives

1. Business Perspective

Focus: Ensures cloud investments accelerate business outcomes and objectives

Key Stakeholders:

  • Business Managers
  • Finance Managers
  • Budget Owners
  • Strategy Stakeholders

Capabilities:

  • Strategy Management
  • Portfolio Management
  • Innovation Management
  • Product Management
  • Financial Management
  • Business Risk Management

Real-World Scenario:

A retail company wants to move its e-commerce platform to AWS to reduce infrastructure costs and improve scalability during sales events. The Business perspective stakeholders would:

  • Build the business case showing ROI
  • Define KPIs to measure success (cost savings, improved uptime)
  • Set strategic priorities for migration (customer-facing systems first)
  • Calculate TCO (Total Cost of Ownership) comparison

2. People Perspective

Focus: Skills, culture, leadership, and organizational structure

Key Stakeholders:

  • Human Resources
  • Staffing
  • People Managers

Capabilities:

  • Resource Management
  • Incentive Management
  • Career Management
  • Training Management
  • Organizational Change Management
  • Culture Evolution

Real-World Scenario:

A financial services firm is moving from traditional infrastructure to cloud-native solutions. The People perspective stakeholders would:

  • Create AWS training programs for existing staff
  • Develop new roles and job descriptions for cloud positions
  • Restructure teams to support DevOps practices
  • Implement change management to address resistance
  • Create incentives for cloud certification

3. Governance Perspective

Focus: Control and oversight for cloud initiatives

Key Stakeholders:

  • CIO
  • Program Managers
  • Enterprise Architects
  • Business Analysts
  • Portfolio Managers

Capabilities:

  • Program and Project Governance
  • Benefit Management
  • Risk Management
  • Cloud Financial Management
  • Data Governance

Real-World Scenario:

A healthcare provider is migrating patient data systems to AWS. The Governance perspective stakeholders would:

  • Establish cloud policies for regulatory compliance (HIPAA)
  • Create IAM policies and role-based access controls
  • Implement AWS Cost Explorer and budgeting alerts
  • Set up AWS Config rules to enforce compliance
  • Define data classification and retention policies

4. Platform Perspective

Focus: Technical infrastructure and applications

Key Stakeholders:

  • CTO
  • Technology Managers
  • Solution Architects

Capabilities:

  • Compute and Network Provisioning
  • Platform Engineering
  • Application Development
  • Data Architecture
  • User Experience Design

Real-World Scenario:

A manufacturing company needs to modernize its legacy production monitoring system. The Platform perspective stakeholders would:

  • Design a microservices architecture using Amazon ECS
  • Set up CI/CD pipelines using AWS CodePipeline
  • Create networking with VPCs, subnets, and security groups
  • Implement data pipelines with Kinesis for real-time analytics
  • Develop infrastructure as code using CloudFormation

5. Security Perspective

Focus: Security, compliance, and risk management

Key Stakeholders:

  • CISO
  • Security Architects
  • IT Security Managers
  • Compliance Specialists

Capabilities:

  • Identity and Access Management
  • Security Governance
  • Threat Detection
  • Vulnerability Management
  • Data Protection
  • Incident Response

Real-World Scenario:

A bank is moving its payment processing systems to AWS. The Security perspective stakeholders would:

  • Implement AWS Shield and WAF for DDoS protection
  • Configure AWS GuardDuty for threat detection
  • Set up Amazon Macie for sensitive data discovery
  • Establish multi-factor authentication with IAM
  • Create encryption policies for data at rest and in transit
  • Develop incident response plans with AWS CloudWatch

6. Operations Perspective

Focus: Health and availability of cloud services

Key Stakeholders:

  • Infrastructure Leaders
  • Operations Managers
  • Site Reliability Engineers

Capabilities:

  • Service Monitoring
  • Application Performance Monitoring
  • Resource Inventory Management
  • Release Management/Change Management
  • Reporting and Analytics
  • Business Continuity/Disaster Recovery
  • IT Service Management

Real-World Scenario:

An e-learning platform experiencing rapid growth needs reliable operations in AWS. The Operations perspective stakeholders would:

  • Set up CloudWatch dashboards for monitoring
  • Configure auto-scaling policies for peak usage times
  • Implement AWS Systems Manager for patch management
  • Create DR plans with cross-region replication
  • Establish runbooks for common operational tasks
  • Use AWS X-Ray for distributed tracing

CAF Implementation Phases

Phase Description Key Activities Real-World Example
Envision Demonstrate how cloud supports business outcomes
  • Identify and prioritize transformation opportunities
  • Evaluate business readiness
  • Identify gaps in capabilities
 A media company conducts workshops with stakeholders to identify pain points in their content delivery workflow, and determines that moving to a cloud-based content management system would improve time-to-market by 60%.
Align Identify capability gaps across the six perspectives
  • Create a communication strategy
  • Develop a cloud operating model
  • Evaluate organizational readiness
 A university IT department discovers they lack CloudOps skills and establishes a training program for existing staff while creating a timeline for migration that aligns with academic calendar milestones.
Launch Implement pilot initiatives in production
  • Implement minimum viable products
  • Test and validate strategies
  • Refine approaches based on learnings
 A retail company migrates their non-critical product catalog to Amazon S3 and CloudFront as a pilot project before planning the migration of their entire e-commerce platform.
Scale Expand production implementations and benefit realization
  • Scale pilots into full implementations
  • Measure and realize business benefits
  • Iterate on improvements
 After successful pilot migration of HR systems, a manufacturing company implements a full-scale migration of all remaining applications using automated pipelines and infrastructure as code practices refined during the pilot phase.

Real-World Cloud Adoption Challenges and CAF Solutions

Challenge CAF Perspective Solution Approach
Lack of cloud skills in IT staff People Create an AWS training roadmap, leverage AWS training partners, and potentially use AWS Managed Services as a bridging solution while building internal capabilities.
Unexpected cloud cost increases Governance/Business Implement AWS Cost Explorer, set up budgeting alerts, use tagging strategies, right-size resources, and implement automated shutdown of development environments outside working hours.
Security concerns with cloud migration Security Use AWS Security Hub, implement least privilege access with IAM, encrypt sensitive data, and leverage AWS compliance programs relevant to your industry.
Existing applications aren't cloud-ready Platform Use the 6 R's strategy (Rehost, Replatform, Repurchase, Refactor, Retire, Retain), starting with simple lift-and-shift and gradually optimizing for cloud.
No clear ROI measurement for cloud Business Define specific KPIs before migration (cost savings, time-to-market improvements, uptime), and use AWS Cost and Usage Reports to track spending against business outcomes.
Outages and performance issues Operations Implement robust monitoring with CloudWatch, set up automated alerts, build redundancy across Availability Zones, and create incident response runbooks.

Key Takeaways for the Exam

  • The CAF helps organizations accelerate their cloud adoption journey and digital transformation
  • Each perspective addresses specific responsibilities and is owned by different stakeholders
  • Business and Governance perspectives focus on business capabilities
  • People and Operations perspectives focus on people capabilities
  • Platform and Security perspectives focus on technical capabilities
  • The four transformation domains (Technology, Process, Organization, Product) describe different areas of business change enabled by cloud adoption
  • Successful cloud adoption requires transformation across all six perspectives
  • The CAF is used to identify gaps in skills and processes that might impede successful cloud adoption
